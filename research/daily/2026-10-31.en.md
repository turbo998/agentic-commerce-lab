## 4. Architecture impact and open items

1. **Finer AI-Native execution semantics:** “a claim row exists” must not be treated as “external action is authorized.”  
   Local coordination state, semantic readback validation, and payment-attempt ledger state must remain separated.
2. **SPT page dynamic-field noise:** future source-monitor interpretation should continue combining body-level diff evidence,
   not equating single hash changes with product releases.
3. **Poppy Draft 0.3 publication gate:** until `0.3` is officially published in `versions.json`, implementation remains anchored
   to published `0.2`, with `0.3` used only for forward-looking architecture analysis and risk tracking.
