### 2.2 Google A2A Python SDK (`a2aproject/a2a-python#1377`, `#1375`, `#1286`, `#1277`, `#1274`, `#1273`, `PR-05..PR-07`)
1. **REST Tenant Routes Mounted Under `{path_prefix}/{tenant}` (`#1377`, `PR-05`)**:
   - Fixes `create_rest_routes` mounting tenant routes at `f'{path_prefix}/{{tenant}}'` with unprefixed `tenant_route_objects` so requests to `/a2a/rest/my-tenant/message:send` resolve `context.tenant == 'my-tenant'` instead of 404ing under `/{tenant}/a2a/rest/...`.
2. **Malformed JSON-RPC Envelope Guard & Extra-Field Error Mapping (`#1375`, `PR-06`)**:
   - Replaces raw `JSONRPC20Response(**response_data)` in `JsonRpcTransport._check_jsonrpc_response` and `_handle_sse_error` so non-dict JSON bodies, objects missing both `result` and `error`, or non-int `error.code` raise `A2AClientError`, while error objects carrying extra fields (e.g., `reason`) still map via `_ERROR_CODE_MAP` (such as `-32001` $\to$ `TaskNotFoundError`), including on `delete_task_push_notification_config`.
3. **Cross-SDK AgentCard JWS Interop Vectors, `application/a2a+json` Media Type & `ignore_unknown_fields` (`#1286`, `#1277`, `#1274`, `#1273`, `PR-07`)**:
   - Adds `signing_interop_vectors.json` (`a2a-card-sign-v01`) verifying identical canonical UTF-8 bytes and `ES256` signatures across `a2a-sdk 1.2.1`, `@a2a-js/sdk 1.3.0`, `a2a-go`, and a reference signer (`S0-001..S0-004` `MUST-ACCEPT`, `S0-REJECT-005` `MUST-REJECT`, `#1286`);
   - Labels HTTP+JSON REST responses with `Content-Type: application/a2a+json` via `A2AJSONResponse` (`#1274`);
   - Enables `ignore_unknown_fields=True` in JSON-RPC and REST protobuf parsing while returning `-32602` with a typed `google.rpc.ErrorInfo` array on structural type mismatches (`#1273`), and preserves `TaskStatus.timestamp`, `icon_url`, `oauth2_metadata_url`, and `skill.security` in `v0_3/proto_utils.py` (`#1277`).
