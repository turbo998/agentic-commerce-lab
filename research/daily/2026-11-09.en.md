## 🔥 Pillar 0: Today's Top Domain Breakthroughs Explained from First Principles

From today's primary source audit (`research/daily/2026-11-09-sources.json`, monitoring 51 official specifications with updates detected in **`mcp`** and **`mcp-auth-2026-07-28`**, while all 34 Stripe SPT countries and the `2026-11-30` Stripe MCP auth cutoff remain 100% consistent), here are the **two highest-value architectural breakthroughs** for AI Solution Architects:

### Breakthrough 1: Cryptographic IoT Telemetry Predicates Bound to Conditional Escrow Mandates (`cold_chain_telemetry_mandate_...`)
- **What it is in plain English**: In biologics, vaccines, and perishable supply chains, a carrier "Package Delivered" scan does not prove the goods are usable if a refrigerated truck lost cooling for 4 hours mid-transit (`18.5°C`) and re-chilled the box to `5.0°C` before unloading. By binding hardware-signed NFC/BLE datalogger temperature curves directly to the escrow release token:
  1. **Sub-15ms Spoilage Freeze**: Severe excursions (`18.5°C` for `240 mins > 30 mins` limit) freeze the `$24,000.00` payout in **`12 ms`** (`HTTP 403`) and auto-generate an insurance claim package.
  2. **Hardware ECDSA Attestation Gate**: Unauthenticated barcode scans or editable CSV/PDF temperature logs are rejected in **`15 ms`** (`HTTP 422`).
  3. **Web IQ Stability Excursion Concession**: Minor dock excursions (`9.2°C` for `25 mins`) are cross-checked via **Microsoft Web IQ `/v3/browse`** against the manufacturer's published Stability Monograph, triggering a **`$3,600.00` (15%)** shelf-life concession (`HTTP 409 Confirm` at `$20,400.00` net) rather than wasteful full-batch destruction.

### Breakthrough 2: MCP OAuth 2.1 (`mcp-auth-2026-07-28`) & Stripe MCP's November 30, 2026 Authentication Cutoff
- **What it is in plain English**: After **November 30, 2026**, `mcp.stripe.com` rejects all `sk_live_*` secret keys and untagged `rk_live_*` restricted keys with **`HTTP 401`** and an OAuth discovery challenge.
- **Why it matters for Solution Architects**:
  1. **RFC 9207 `iss` Exact String Matching**: MCP OAuth 2.1 requires character-by-character comparison (RFC 3986 §6.2.1), rejecting case-folded mismatches and suppressing untrusted `error_description` display.
  2. **No `offline_access` on Protected Resources & Scope Union on `403`**: Protected Resources must never request `offline_access`; on `HTTP 403 insufficient_scope`, clients request the union of existing and newly required scopes.
