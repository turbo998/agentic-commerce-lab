## 🔥 Pillar 0: Today's Top Domain Breakthroughs Explained from First Principles

From today's primary source audit (`research/daily/2026-11-08-sources.json`, monitoring 51 official specifications with 4 changed sources: **`stripe-spt-connect`**, **`stripe-ai-skills`**, **`mcp`**, and **`mcp-auth-2026-07-28`**, and 34 SPT countries verified consistent), here are the **two highest-value architectural breakthroughs** for AI Solution Architects:

### Breakthrough 1: Stripe Connect Multi-Party Marketplace SPT Routing (`shared-payment-tokens?agent-seller=connect`) & Asymmetric 4s Seller Hooks
- **What it is in plain English**: When an AI agent buys from a multi-vendor marketplace or SaaS platform (like Shopify, DoorDash, or a B2B procurement portal) rather than a single standalone store, how do funds flow safely from the buyer's scoped payment token (`spt_...`) to both the platform and the sub-merchant?
- **Why it matters for Solution Architects**:
  1. **Three Connect Charge Models Supported**: Covers `Direct Charges`, `Destination Charges`, and `Separate Charges and Transfers` via `network_business_profile`.
  2. **The `Stripe-Account` Header Trap**: On Destination Charges or Separate Charges & Transfers **without `on_behalf_of`**, the platform must create the `PaymentIntent` using its own `profile_...` ID and **must omit the `Stripe-Account` header**. Passing `Stripe-Account` without `on_behalf_of` causes an account-ownership mismatch error.
  3. **Asymmetric 4-Second Seller Hook SLA**: Seller webhooks (`v1.delegated_checkout.*`) enforce a strict `4,000 ms` timeout, but behave asymmetrically by design:
     - If `product_price_availability` times out (`> 4s`), Stripe **falls back to the seller's imported Catalog Feed (`upsert`) and continues checkout**;
     - If `finalize_checkout` times out (`> 4s`), Stripe **immediately declines the payment** to protect seller risk controls.

### Breakthrough 2: MCP OAuth 2.1 (`mcp-auth-2026-07-28`) & Stripe MCP's November 30, 2026 Authentication Cutoff
- **What it is in plain English**: Early MCP adopters often pasted full-access merchant secret keys (`sk_live_*`) into local agent configs. Starting December 2026 (after **November 30, 2026**), `mcp.stripe.com` rejects all `sk_live_*` secret keys and untagged `rk_live_*` restricted keys with **`HTTP 401`** and an OAuth discovery challenge.
- **Why it matters for Solution Architects**:
  1. **RFC 9207 `iss` Exact String Matching**: MCP OAuth 2.1 requires clients to validate the authorization callback `iss` parameter using **RFC 3986 §6.2.1 simple character-by-character comparison** (rejecting case-folded mismatches like `https://AUTH.merchant.example` and suppressing unverified `error_description` rendering).
  2. **No `offline_access` on Protected Resources & Scope Union on `403`**: Protected Resources must never request `offline_access`; on `HTTP 403 insufficient_scope`, clients request the union of existing and newly required scopes (`mcp:tools ∪ commerce:checkout`).
  3. **Human Confirmation on High-Consequence Tools**: `create_refund` and Treasury tools (`convert_currency`, `send_money`, `transfer_money`) always pause for explicit human confirmation (`Confirm`).
